GOOD TO KNOW
Clarity, before
you begin.
Made for exploration.
Honest about its limits.

      
Can I use this for real patient records?
No. Sureword is a challenge research prototype using fictional role-play data. It is not approved for diagnosis, triage, or live medical records. This demo has no user authentication; never enter personal or patient information.

      
Which languages can I speak?
The app offers Hindi, Indian English, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, and Odia. The workspace controls are localized; this overview is in English. Live speech requires configured Gnani credentials.

      
What happens to my audio and records?
The server processes temporary audio and removes it after processing. Committed records use the server’s SQLite database. On Vercel, the temporary filesystem is not durable or shared storage. CSV and JSON exports are available in the registry.

      
Do the samples call a speech provider?
No. Sample buttons supply labeled simulated transcripts to the parsing workflow. They let you explore structured extraction without recording audio or calling a paid provider.

      
Where can I explore the project?
Sureword is MIT licensed. Explore the source repository or the running server’s API documentation. No paid plans or subscription are offered here.
